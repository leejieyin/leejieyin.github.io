NewJeans

NewJeans is a South Korean girl group that debuted in 2022 under ADOR. The group has five members: Minji, Hanni, Danielle, Haerin, and Hyein. They became well known for their unique music style and simple, fresh image. Their music is mainly based on K-pop, with influences from different types of pop music. Some of their popular songs include “Attention”, “Hype Boy”, “Ditto”, “OMG”, and “Super Shy”. NewJeans has gained popularity both in South Korea and internationally. Their songs are known for their catchy melodies and easy-listening style. The group has also received several awards and has become one of the well-known K-pop groups of their generation.
